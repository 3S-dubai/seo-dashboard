What is the difference between Mainland and Free Zone?
+
A Mainland company can operate anywhere in the UAE and trade directly with the local market. A Free Zone company offers 100% foreign ownership and tax benefits but is generally restricted to operating within the free zone or internationally.
How long does company setup take?
+
Free zone setup can take as little as 1-3 business days. Mainland setup typically takes 5-10 business days depending on the activity and required approvals. Our team works to ensure the fastest possible processing.
Can a foreigner own 100% of a UAE company?
+
Yes. All UAE free zones allow 100% foreign ownership. Following recent reforms, many mainland activities also permit 100% foreign ownership without a local sponsor. Our team advises on the specific rules for your chosen activity.
Can I open a UAE corporate bank account after setup?
+
Yes. After your setup is complete we guide you through bank account preparation, required documents and suitable bank options based on your business type and profile — at no extra charge.
Do I need a physical office to set up in UAE?
+
Not always. Many free zones offer virtual offices and flexi desks that satisfy licensing requirements at a fraction of the cost. Physical offices are required for some mainland activities — we'll advise the most cost-effective option for your needs.
What is the renewal cost after the first year?
+
Renewal costs may vary based on the selected jurisdiction and package. We show you the expected renewal cost upfront before you commit — no surprises later.
