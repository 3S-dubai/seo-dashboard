What documents do I need for a trade license?
+
You need a passport copy, a copy of your UAE residence or tourist visa and Emirates ID, and a No Objection Certificate (NOC) from your current sponsor if you are a UAE resident.
What is the difference between Mainland and Free Zone?
+
A Mainland company can operate anywhere in the UAE and trade directly with the local market. A Free Zone company offers 100% foreign ownership and tax benefits but is generally restricted to operating within the free zone or internationally — with some exceptions depending on the zone.
How long does company setup take?
+
Free zone company setup can take as little as 1–3 business days. Mainland setup typically takes 5–10 business days depending on the activity and required approvals. Our team works to ensure the fastest possible processing at every step.
Can a foreigner own 100% of a UAE company?
+
Yes. All UAE free zones allow 100% foreign ownership. Following recent reforms, many mainland activities also now permit 100% foreign ownership without a local sponsor. Our team will advise on the specific rules for your chosen activity.
What is the UAE Golden Visa and who qualifies?
+
The UAE Golden Visa is a long-term residence visa (5 or 10 years) available to investors, entrepreneurs, specialised talent, scientists, outstanding students, and humanitarian pioneers. Eligibility is based on investment value, professional achievement, or academic standing.
Can I open a UAE corporate bank account as a foreigner?
+
Yes. Many UAE banks accept 100% foreign-owned companies, particularly those set up in recognised free zones. We have strong relationships with leading UAE banks and match your business profile to the most suitable option to maximise approval chances.
Do I need a physical office to set up in UAE?
+
Not always. Many free zones offer virtual offices and flexi desks that satisfy licensing requirements at a fraction of the cost of a full office. Physical offices are required for some mainland activities. We'll guide you to the most cost-effective solution for your needs.
